# Drone Pulse Labs
### Skies of the Future

Drone Pulse Labs is an engineering collective dedicated to building advanced software solutions for drones, Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs), and autonomous systems. We specialize in robust flight control software, intelligent automation pipelines, and custom payload integrations designed to push the boundaries of aerial robotics.

Our main goal is to make life easier for the current industry while allowing more young minds to enter this industry by making things much easier and attractive than they are now. We do the dirty work and handle the nightmare backend and let the users enjoy the colorful frontend!

Beyond internal R&D, we offer engineering consultations, architecture reviews, and strategic technical guidance to budding drone systems engineers and early-stage startups.

---

## Core Focus Areas

* **UAV Software Engineering**: Custom flight control configurations, custom GCS (Ground Control Station) applications, and embedded systems programming.
* **Autonomous Navigation**: Obstacle avoidance algorithms, automated path planning, and sensory-data fusion architectures.
* **Consulting & Strategy**: Technical advisory for drone startups, hardware-software integration troubleshooting, and compliance-aware system designs.

## Repositories & Ecosystem

Our open-source repositories generally fall under three primary categories:

1. **Autonomous Frameworks**: Core algorithmic models, simulation environments, and ROS/ROS2 nodes for autonomous operations.
2. **Firmware Modifications**: Production-ready configurations and patches for open-source autopilot stacks.
3. **Developer Tools**: Internal APIs, automation utilities, and diagnostic scripts used to streamline drone testing pipelines.

## Collaboration & Consulting

We actively collaborate with hardware manufacturers, software teams, and research groups. If you are a startup looking to accelerate your UAV automation roadmap or an engineer seeking architectural advice, we offer consultation in the following formats:

* Architecture & Code Reviews
* Hardware-Software Interfacing Strategies
